2. SYNTHESIS OF SCIENTIFIC FINDINGS & LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

1. The "Fixed Combined" Paradox (Proof that Gradient Variance != Mitigation):
   - At L=8, Fixed Combined displayed the highest initial gradient variance (0.0347),
     exceeding Baseline (0.0210) and Controlled Init (0.0118).
   - However, Fixed Combined achieved almost ZERO optimization progress (mean ΔC = +0.0610).
   - Cause: Freezing prior layers while initializing active layers near identity confines
     optimization to an affine subspace that cannot rotate the frozen global state toward the ground state.
   - Core Scientific Lesson: Elevating gradient variance is a necessary condition for escaping barren plateaus,
     but NOT a sufficient condition for optimization convergence.

2. Full-Circuit vs Layerwise Expressibility Bottleneck:
   - Full-circuit Controlled Init optimizes effectively (reaching C_final = -0.8846 at L=8).
   - Layerwise training (Arms 3, 4, 5, 6) freezes earlier parameters; while computationally cheaper,
     frozen layers constrain the expressible trajectory and can lead to early optimization plateaus.
