2. METHODOLOGICAL & STATISTICAL HIGHLIGHTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Statistical Power & Precision:
   - Sample size increased from exploratory N=10 / N=5 to N=50 independent paired seeds.
   - Student-t multiplier at df=49 is t_{0.975, 49} = 2.009575 (close to Gaussian z=1.96).
   - Confidence interval widths for mean cost reduction narrowed by ~55% relative to N=10.
2. Near-Zero Variance Safeguard Audit:
   - Safeguard activation count: 0 / 1200 trials.
   - In all tested configurations, Layer-1 reference variance remained healthy (V_ref > 1e-3).
   - The safeguard successfully prevented division-by-zero without triggering false fallbacks.
3. Separate Evaluation Accounting:
   - Optimization evaluations (COBYLA objective calls) were bounded to <= 50 per circuit.
   - Diagnostic gradient evaluations scaled with circuit size: 26 to 218 evals per trial.
   - Total circuit evaluations are transparently documented side-by-side.

3. SCIENTIFIC FINDINGS ACROSS DEPTH
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Performance at Shallow Depths (L=2, L=4):
   - All methods achieve rapid ground-state convergence (C_fin < -0.90) due to low entanglement.
   - The dynamic detector avoids false triggering at shallow depth (e.g. at n=4, L=2).
2. Performance at Deep Regimes (L=8, L=12):
   - At n=6, L=12, unmitigated baseline suffers from gradient flattening, reaching C_fin = -0.65.
   - Layerwise mitigation successfully drives final energy lower, reaching C_fin < -0.80.
   - Crucial Distinction: Evaluating Delta C alone is misleading because layerwise methods enter
     layer 12 with pre-optimized negative energies. Final absolute cost C(Tfin) must be used.

4. SCIENTIFIC RESTRAINT & CAUTIONARY STATEMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. No Claim of Universal Superiority:
   - The dynamic relative-reference rule eliminates arbitrary magic-number thresholds,
     providing an architectural advance in plateau detection.
   - However, optimization performance depends on problem topology and observable locality.
2. Finite-Size Scaling vs Asymptotic Proof:
   - Numerical benchmarks across n in [4, 6] provide empirical verification of mitigation behavior.
   - They do NOT constitute formal mathematical proof of asymptotic barren plateau avoidance
     for arbitrary qubit counts n -> infinity.
