4. SCIENTIFIC FINDINGS & INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Full-Circuit Controlled Init (Group 2): Confirms that near-identity initialization
   dramatically preserves gradient variance at depth 20 without requiring layer-wise freezing.
2. Pure Layer-Wise (Group 3): Confirms that appending uniform random layers onto a frozen
   unitary still succumbs to barren plateau decay at L >= 8.
3. Fixed Combined (Group 4): Demonstrates steady gradient trainability when layer-wise
   growth is paired with near-identity initialization.
4. Dynamic Adaptive Trigger (Group 5): Confirms that dynamically triggering mitigation
   avoids early constraint while successfully rescuing circuits when gradients collapse.
5. Guardrail: Preserving non-zero gradient variance proves cost landscape trainability,
   but does NOT prove convergence to the global minimum of a target Hamiltonian.
